How to read this

● Cashed, not in eCW
Blue Shield shows the cheque was cashed, but eCW has no payment under that number. Enter the payment in eCW.
● Entered, unposted
The payment exists in eCW but part of it was never applied to claims. Finish posting it.
● No copy of cheque
A cashed cheque with no image in the SharePoint folder. Scan it into Insurance Checks.
● Amounts differ
The copy, Blue Shield and eCW do not agree on the amount. Check which one is right.
● Posted
In eCW, fully applied, copy on file, amounts agree. Nothing to do.
● Not cashed yet
Blue Shield issued it but the bank has not cashed it. Wait.
● Copy only
We hold a copy but Blue Shield does not list this cheque: another payer (Aetna, Health Net…) or outside the search window. Not tracked here.
