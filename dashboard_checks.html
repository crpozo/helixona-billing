How to read this

● Cashed, not in eCW
Blue Shield shows the check was cashed, but eCW has no payment under that number. Enter the payment in eCW.
● Not in eCW
A scanned check from another payer (Cigna, Aetna, Health Net…) that eCW has no payment for. If it is not in eCW, we do not have it. Enter the payment in eCW.
● Entered, unposted
The payment exists in eCW but part of it was never applied to claims. Finish posting it.
● No copy of check
A cashed check with no image in the SharePoint folder. Scan it into Insurance Checks.
● Amounts differ
The copy, Blue Shield and eCW do not agree on the amount. Check which one is right.
● Posted
In eCW, fully applied, copy on file, amounts agree. Nothing to do.
● Not cashed yet
Blue Shield issued it but the bank has not cashed it. Wait.
● Other payer
Blue Shield does not list this check (another insurer, or outside the search window), so only eCW can vouch for it. Informational; the verdict comes from eCW.
● eCW not checked
eCW could not be read on the last run. Nothing can be said about these until the next run.
